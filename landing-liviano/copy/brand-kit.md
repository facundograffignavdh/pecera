# Pecera — Brand Kit

> Source of truth for the Pecera landing page and campaign. Items marked **(proposed)** were not supplied and are suggestions for you to confirm.

## 1. Brand name
**Pecera** (Spanish for "fish tank"). Always lowercase in the wordmark: `pecera`.

## 2. Brand slogan
**Build in Public.**
Supporting line (proposed): *El acuario donde las startups se hacen visibles.*

## 3. Brand positioning
Pecera is a short-form video and networking platform for the startup ecosystem. Think "Shark Tank meets TikTok":

- **Founders** pitch their startup in short vertical videos.
- **Investors** discover, follow and invest.
- **Strategic allies** (agencies, dev shops, legal, design, etc.) sell their services directly to startups.
- Every startup keeps a **public progress tracker**, which gives the ecosystem real transparency.
- A **physical NFC business card** and **events and hackathons** bridge online discovery with real-world connection.

Positioning statement: *Pecera is the transparent, public home of the startup ecosystem. It is the place where building in public turns into funding, partners and contacts.*

What it replaces: scattered pitch decks, cold DMs, opaque fundraising and closed networks.

## 4. Target audience
| Segment | What they want from Pecera |
|---|---|
| **Startups / founders** (primary) | Visibility, validation, funding, a network |
| **Venture capital / investors** | Deal flow with verifiable traction |
| **Entrepreneurs** (pre-startup) | Community, learning, first connections |
| **Strategic allies / service providers** | Qualified leads from startups that need them |
| **Event organizers / hackathon communities** (secondary) | A hub for participants and projects |

Market: Argentina and LatAm first, Spanish-speaking. Page copy should be primarily in Spanish.

## 5. Brand personality
**Premium, modern, minimalist, finance-grade.** Trustworthy like a fintech, warm like a community.

- Confident, not loud
- Transparent and honest (the "Build in Public" ethos)
- Clean and precise, with a lot of white space
- Ambitious but approachable
- Playful only through the water and fish metaphor, never through clutter

Voice: direct, short sentences, founder-to-founder. Avoid corporate jargon and hype.

## 6. Color palette
Orange on light is the core of the identity, with black as the high-contrast alternate.

| Role | Name | Hex |

Rules:
- Default theme is **light**: white and warm mist surfaces with orange as the single strong accent.
- Dark sections are allowed for contrast moments, using ink black with orange and white.
- Orange text on white is only for large display type. Use ink black for body copy (contrast).

## 7. Typography direction
- **Display / headlines:** **Fraunces** (Google Fonts). Use heavy weights (700–900) with optical size on, matching the soft, bold serif of the `pecera` wordmark.
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** (Google Fonts), a clean sans-serif. You listed "Georgia / Sans Serif": Georgia is a system font, so it is the fallback.
- Font stacks:
  - Headlines: `'Fraunces', Georgia, serif`
  - Body: `'Inter', system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`
- Scale: very large hero type (clamp ~56–120px), generous line-height on body (1.6), tight tracking on display.

## 9. Product description
Pecera is a social platform for startups, built around short-form video.

**Core features**
1. **Video pitches (Shark Tank × TikTok):** vertical short-form feed where entrepreneurs pitch in under a minute or two.
2. **Investor layer:** investors browse, shortlist, follow and invest in startups.
3. **Allies marketplace:** strategic partners present their services to startups inside the same feed and network.
4. **Startup networking (social network):** profiles, follows, messaging, introductions.
5. **Progress tracking and company transparency:** startups publish metrics, milestones and updates, so traction is visible and verifiable.
6. **NFC business card:** a physical tap-to-share card that opens your Pecera profile.
7. **Events and hackathons:** a space to run, join and showcase events, with projects and teams linked to profiles.

**Design of the product experience:** minimalist, finance-like interface, light surfaces, orange accents, subtle water motion.

## 10. Key benefits
Use these as feature cards and section headlines.

- **Pitch in public.** Show your startup in a short video and let the ecosystem come to you.
- **Get funded.** Investors discover founders by traction, not by who they know.
- **Find your allies.** Service providers who already understand startups, one tap away.
- **Network that works.** Real startup connections, not vanity followers.
- **Prove your progress.** A public tracker builds trust before the first meeting.
- **Meet in real life.** NFC card and hackathons connect the online and offline worlds.
- **Radical transparency.** Companies show how they are really doing.

## 11. Visual mood
- **Overall:** minimalist, finance-like, light and orange. An aquarium reimagined as a premium fintech.
- **Lighting:** bright, soft, diffused daylight, with gentle caustics (light ripples) as the only "effect". Orange as warm glow.
- **Materials and textures:** clean glass, water, subtle ripples, matte white surfaces, soft orange gradients. No heavy textures.
- **Background:** white and warm mist, with generous negative space. Occasional full-bleed orange or ink-black blocks for rhythm.
- **Imagery:** a stylized orange fish (echoing the logo's two halves), a glass fish tank (*pecera*), bubbles, water surface. Keep it minimal and graphic, never cartoonish.
- **Motion:** slow, fluid and calm. Bubbles drift up, fish glide, content fades and slides in with soft easing. No abrupt or flashy transitions.
- **Camera style (video):** slow macro and gentle dolly moves, shallow depth of field, clean compositions.
- **Atmosphere:** transparent, optimistic, premium, alive.

## 12. Website goal
A scroll-driven landing page that:
1. **Explains the product clearly** (what Pecera is and how it works).
2. **Creates desire** for founders, investors and allies.
3. **Prospects and captures contacts:** the primary conversion is **waitlist / early-access sign-up** and contact capture (email, role, startup).

Primary CTA: *Unirme a la pecera* (join the waitlist) (proposed). Secondary CTA: *Ver cómo funciona*.

Success metrics: sign-ups, segment split (founder / investor / ally), scroll depth to the "How it works" section.

## 14. Higgsfield video brief
*Adapted to Pecera. The original template was for headphones, and this brand has no physical hero product, so the hero subject is the brand metaphor: the fish tank.*

**Purpose:** a looping cinematic background video for the landing hero, usable behind text on a light layout.

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 6–10s seamless loop, 24 fps, no text, no logos, no people.

**Concept:** a pristine, minimalist glass fish tank on a clean white studio surface, filled with crystal-clear water. Two stylized orange fish glide slowly in opposite directions, echoing the interlocking shapes of the Pecera symbol. Soft bubbles rise. The mood is premium and calm, like a fintech brand film.

**Scene and subject**
- A rectangular, ultra-clear glass tank, centered or on the right third (leave the left 50% empty for headline copy).
- Two sleek orange fish (colors `#F87C43` and a lighter salmon `#F48C62`) moving in gentle opposing arcs, never touching.
- Minimal aquascape: no gravel clutter, maybe one smooth white stone. Almost empty, for a graphic look.
- Fine bubbles drifting upward, plus very subtle floating particles that suggest data or growth (small, soft, not neon).

**Lighting**
- Bright, soft, diffused daylight from above and the side.
- Gentle water caustics (light ripples) projected on the white surface and tank walls.
- Warm orange reflections and glow, high key, no dark shadows.

**Materials**
- Clear glass with delicate edge highlights, water with subtle refraction, matte white surface, glossy fish scales with a soft sheen.

**Camera**
- Slow macro push-in (about 5–8% over the clip) with a very subtle lateral drift. Shallow depth of field, with the fish in focus and the background softly blurred.
- Smooth, stabilized, no handheld shake.

**Color grade**
- Dominant: white and warm mist, with orange as the single accent. A faint aqua tint in the water (`#E8F4F6`) is allowed.
- Clean, bright, slightly lifted blacks, finance-like restraint.

**Motion:** slow and fluid. Loop must be seamless (the end frame matches the start frame).

**Avoid:** cartoon or mascot styling, neon, dark moody lighting, text, watermarks, any real brand or logo, cluttered aquarium decor, people or hands.

**Prompt (copy-ready):**
> Cinematic minimalist hero shot of a pristine glass fish tank on a clean white studio surface, crystal-clear water, two sleek orange fish gliding slowly in opposite directions, fine bubbles rising, soft diffused daylight with gentle water caustics, warm orange reflections, premium fintech aesthetic, high-key bright palette of white and vivid orange (#F87C43), shallow depth of field, slow macro push-in, seamless loop, 16:9, no text, no logos.

**Optional second clip (later):** a slow macro of the water surface with ripples and orange light, for use behind the final CTA section.
